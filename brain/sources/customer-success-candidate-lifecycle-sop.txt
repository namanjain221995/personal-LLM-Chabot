1. PROCESS PURPOSE
================================================================================

The candidate lifecycle is organized into seven business phases:

Phase 1 - Screening + Intake
Phase 2 - Payment + Onboarding Setup
Phase 3 - Resume Process (Before Training)
Phase 4 - Training
Phase 5 - Out-of-Training Confirmation + Marketing Start Handoff
Phase 6 - Interview Operations + Readiness + Rejection Training
Phase 7 - Offer Stage + ACH Confirmation + Credential Release

The process is cross-functional. Customer Success (CS) is a major coordination and
handoff owner, but Sales, Marketing, QA, Training, Interview Readiness, and Rejection
Training teams own specific steps.

The end-to-end business objective is to move an eligible candidate from screening,
through onboarding and training, into marketing and interviews, and finally through
the offer-stage controls, while stopping or closing the candidate when required.


================================================================================
2. CORE ACTORS AND RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

CANDIDATE
- Person moving through the lifecycle.
- Participates in screening/intake, onboarding, resume review, training, interviews,
  rejection coaching when needed, and offer-stage actions.

SALES
- Performs the initial screening call.
- Decides whether the candidate passes the Sales approval gate.
- Collects upfront payment after Marketing intake approval.

MARKETING
- Performs the intake interview and approval gate in Phase 1.
- Creates and updates the resume in Phase 3.
- Starts marketing after CS hands over a ready candidate in Phase 5.
- Confirms candidate availability for readiness/rejection sessions in Phase 6.
- Changes offer-stage email credentials immediately in Phase 7.
- Shares new credentials only after CS confirms ACH is signed.

CUSTOMER SUCCESS (CS)
- Owns major onboarding, expectation-setting, coordination, and lifecycle handoffs.
- Handles/coordinates the service agreement, onboarding document, and required
  candidate information after payment.
- Explains the end-to-end process, expectations, and rules.
- Creates the Marketing WhatsApp group.
- Confirms coursework and timeline before resume work.
- Raises the resume request to Marketing.
- Creates the training group after resume approval.
- Receives training pass/fail information from QA.
- Confirms out-of-training status, performs a final audit/checklist, and hands the
  candidate to Marketing as ready to start marketing.
- In the offer stage, confirms whether ACH is signed before credentials may be
  released to the candidate.

QA
- Receives Week 1 and Week 2 interview-panel scores during training.
- Communicates Pass/Fail to CS for each training track.
- Confirms to CS that a candidate is out of training before the Phase 5 handoff.

TRAINING TEAM
- Runs training.
- Training deliverables must meet the required threshold.
- Supports the Week 1 and Week 2 training tracks.
- A failed final training result gets one additional chance to repeat the training
  plan once.

INTERVIEW READINESS TEAM
- Runs an interview-readiness session when readiness training is required before an
  interview.

REJECTION TRAINING TEAM
- Runs a rejection-training session after a candidate is rejected or ghosted, when
  the process routes the candidate into that recovery path.

AIC PORTAL
- Used to raise the interview request in Phase 6.


================================================================================
3. END-TO-END LIFECYCLE SUMMARY
================================================================================

The normal success path is:

Sales screening
-> Sales approval
-> Marketing intake interview
-> Marketing approval
-> Upfront payment collection
-> CS onboarding setup
-> CS expectation-setting
-> Marketing WhatsApp group
-> Resume creation / review / approval
-> Training group
-> Week 1 / Week 2 training evaluation
-> Final training pass
-> CS out-of-training audit
-> Marketing start
-> Interview request(s)
-> Interview readiness when needed
-> Interview(s)
-> Next rounds as applicable
-> Offer received
-> Marketing changes email credentials
-> CS verifies ACH signed
-> New credentials shared to candidate
-> End

Common non-success/recovery paths include:
- Sales rejects candidate -> stop/close candidate and log reason.
- Marketing rejects candidate -> stop/close candidate and log reason.
- Training final result fails -> one more chance; repeat training plan once.
- Interview outcome is rejected/ghosted -> rejection-training flow.
- Post-rejection decision can either return the candidate to interview flow or
  stop/close the candidate and log reason.
- ACH is not signed -> continue waiting/follow-up; do not share new credentials.

4. PHASE 1 - SCREENING + INTAKE PROCESS FLOW
================================================================================

ENTRY CONDITION
- Candidate enters the process at Start.

PRIMARY OWNERS
- Sales first.
- Marketing second.

PROCESS
1. Sales conducts a screening call.
2. The purpose of the screening call is to check candidate criteria fit.
3. Decision: Does Sales approve the candidate?

IF SALES = YES
4. Candidate moves to a Marketing intake interview.
5. Decision: Does Marketing approve the candidate?

IF MARKETING = YES
6. Candidate proceeds to Phase 2.

IF SALES = NO
- Stop/close the candidate.
- Log the reason.
- Process ends for that candidate.

IF MARKETING = NO
- Stop/close the candidate.
- Log the reason.
- Process ends for that candidate.

CUSTOMER SUCCESS INVOLVEMENT
- No explicit CS action is shown in Phase 1.
- CS begins explicit operational ownership in Phase 2 after payment.

PHASE 1 BUSINESS RULES
- Both approval gates are mandatory for the normal path.
- A rejection at either gate terminates the active process.
- The closure reason must be logged.

6. PHASE 3 - RESUME PROCESS (BEFORE TRAINING)
================================================================================

ENTRY CONDITION
- Phase 2 onboarding completed and candidate enters the resume process.

PRIMARY OWNERS
- CS initiates and coordinates.
- Marketing creates/updates the resume.
- Candidate can request changes.

PROCESS
1. CS confirms coursework and timeline.
2. CS -> Marketing: raise resume request.
3. Marketing creates the resume.
4. Marketing shares the resume in the WhatsApp group.
5. Decision: Does the candidate request changes?

IF CANDIDATE REQUESTS CHANGES = YES
6. Marketing conducts a Zoom call and notes requested changes.
7. Marketing performs a one-time resume update.
8. Resume becomes auto-approved after the one update.

IF CANDIDATE REQUESTS CHANGES = NO
6. Resume proceeds to auto-approved path.

AUTO-APPROVAL RULES SHOWN IN THE SOP
- Auto-approve in 24 hours if there is no response.
- Auto-approve after one update.

AFTER RESUME APPROVAL
9. CS creates the training group.
10. Candidate proceeds to Phase 4.

CS PURPOSE IN THIS PHASE
- Validate pre-resume readiness inputs such as coursework/timeline.
- Trigger the Marketing resume workflow.
- Ensure the approved resume transitions the candidate into training.

IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULES
- Candidate feedback is not an unlimited revision loop.
- The SOP explicitly shows a one-time resume update.
- No response for 24 hours leads to auto-approval.
- After one update, the resume is auto-approved.

NOT SPECIFIED BY SOURCE
- What "coursework" must contain.
- Exact timeline fields.
- Resume template or scoring criteria.
- What qualifies as a change request.
- Salesforce object/field that represents resume approval.

7. PHASE 4 - TRAINING
================================================================================

ENTRY CONDITION
- Training begins from the Phase 3 training group.

PRIMARY OWNERS
- Training Team.
- Interview panel participants.
- QA for score review / pass-fail communication.
- CS receives pass/fail communication from QA.

PROCESS OVERVIEW
1. Training Team runs training.
2. Training deliverables must meet the required threshold.
3. The SOP shows a Week 1 track and a Week 2 track.

WEEK 1 TRACK
- Interview panel interviews occur.
- Scores are submitted to QA.
- QA -> CS communicates Pass/Fail for Week 1.

WEEK 2 TRACK
- Interview panel interviews occur.
- Scores are submitted to QA.
- QA -> CS communicates Pass/Fail for Week 2.

FINAL TRAINING RESULT
- The Week 1 and Week 2 tracks feed a final training result decision.

IF FINAL TRAINING RESULT = PASS
- Exit Phase 4.
- Proceed to Phase 5: Out-of-Training Confirmation.

IF FINAL TRAINING RESULT = FAIL
- Candidate gets one more chance.
- Repeat the training plan once.
- The retry loops the candidate back into training.

IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULE
- The SOP shows one additional training chance, not unlimited retries.

CS PURPOSE IN THIS PHASE
- CS is the recipient of QA pass/fail status and participates in the lifecycle handoff.
- CS is not shown as the team that runs the training itself.

- Exact deliverable threshold.
- Exact scoring formula.
- Exact definition of final training result.
- Whether Week 1 and Week 2 are always mandatory for every candidate.
- Exact behavior after a failed retry beyond the diagram's one-more-chance rule.
- Salesforce fields/objects for training scores and status.

IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULE

INTERVIEW REQUEST

READINESS DECISION

IF READINESS TRAINING = YES
4. Marketing confirms candidate availability.
5. Interview Readiness Team conducts a session with Candidate + Team.
6. Candidate returns to the interview flow.
7. Interview happens.

IF READINESS TRAINING IS NOT NEEDED

INTERVIEW OUTCOME

OUTCOME = NEXT ROUND

IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULES

10. PHASE 7 - OFFER STAGE + ACH CONFIRMATION + CREDENTIAL RELEASE
================================================================================

ENTRY CONDITION
- Candidate is in Offer Stage and an offer is received.

PRIMARY OWNERS
- Marketing changes and later shares email credentials.
- CS confirms ACH signing status.

PROCESS
1. Offer is received.
2. Marketing changes email credentials immediately.
3. Wait/follow up with CS.
4. During this waiting period, do NOT share the new credentials yet.
5. Decision: Does CS confirm ACH is signed?

IF ACH SIGNED = YES
6. Share the new credentials with the candidate.
7. End.

IF ACH SIGNED = NO
- Return to the wait/follow-up state.
- Continue to withhold new credentials.
- Do not release the new credentials until CS confirms ACH is signed.

CRITICAL CONTROL RULE
- Credential release is gated by CS confirmation that ACH is signed.

MARKETING / CS SEPARATION OF DUTIES IN THIS PHASE
- Marketing performs the credential change.
- CS provides the ACH signed confirmation.
- Marketing shares the new credentials only after that confirmation.

NOT SPECIFIED BY SOURCE
- What ACH stands for in this SOP.
- ACH document content or signing mechanism.
- Where ACH status is stored.
- How credentials are generated, secured, or transmitted.
- Salesforce object/field used for offer stage or ACH confirmation.

13. CANONICAL DECISION RULES
================================================================================

RULE P1-SALES-APPROVAL
IF Sales does not approve candidate
THEN stop/close candidate AND log reason.

RULE P1-MARKETING-APPROVAL
IF Marketing does not approve candidate
THEN stop/close candidate AND log reason.

RULE P1-TO-P2
IF Sales approves AND Marketing approves
THEN proceed to Phase 2.

RULE P2-ONBOARDING
AFTER upfront payment
THEN CS completes agreement/document/information onboarding requirements,
explains expectations/rules, and creates Marketing WhatsApp group.

RULE P3-NO-RESPONSE
IF candidate does not respond to resume review within 24 hours
THEN resume is auto-approved.

RULE P3-ONE-UPDATE
IF candidate requests changes
THEN Marketing performs a one-time resume update
AND resume is auto-approved after that update.

RULE P3-TO-P4
AFTER resume approval
THEN CS creates training group
AND candidate proceeds to training.

RULE P4-PASS
IF final training result is Pass
THEN proceed to Phase 5.

RULE P4-FAIL
IF final training result is Fail
THEN candidate gets one more chance
AND repeats the training plan once.

RULE P5-START-MARKETING
IF QA confirms candidate is Out of Training
AND CS final audit is complete
THEN CS submits candidate to Marketing as Ready to Start Marketing
AND Marketing starts marketing.

RULE P6-READINESS
IF interview readiness training is needed
THEN Marketing confirms candidate availability
AND Interview Readiness Team runs session
BEFORE interview flow continues.

RULE P6-REJECTED-GHOSTED
IF interview outcome is Rejected/Ghosted
THEN Marketing confirms availability for Candidate + Rejection Team
AND Rejection Training Team runs session
THEN candidate either returns to flow OR is stopped/closed with reason logged.

RULE P7-CREDENTIAL-GATE
IF CS has NOT confirmed ACH signed
THEN do not share new credentials.

RULE P7-CREDENTIAL-RELEASE
IF CS confirms ACH signed
THEN Marketing can share new credentials with candidate.

After training passes, QA confirms Out of Training to CS, CS performs one final audit,
and CS submits the candidate to Marketing as Ready to Start Marketing.

FACT 14
Marketing then starts marketing; interviews are expected within 1-2 weeks.

FACT 15
Phase 6 uses the AIC Portal to raise interview requests.

FACT 16
If interview readiness training is needed, Marketing confirms candidate availability
and the Interview Readiness Team conducts a session before the interview flow continues.

FACT 17
If the interview outcome is rejected/ghosted, Marketing confirms availability and the
Rejection Training Team conducts a session.

FACT 18
After rejection training, the candidate can return to interview flow or be stopped/
closed with a logged reason.

FACT 19
When an offer is received, Marketing changes email credentials immediately but does
not share the new credentials yet.

FACT 20
New credentials can be shared only after CS confirms ACH is signed.
