1. PURPOSE & HOW TO READ THIS
================================================================================
This file is a single, self-contained reference describing the Salesforce
Invoice/Payment system and its QuickBooks Online (QB) integration in full detail:
every field on the two custom objects (Invoice__c, Payment__c), the exact formula
and roll-up logic, all picklist values, validation rules, configuration, the
automation (Flows), the QB endpoints, and all business rules.

Conventions used below:
  [F]  = a formula field (read-only, computed).
  [R]  = a roll-up summary field (aggregates child Payment records).
  otherwise the field is a normal stored/editable field.
  "EMI" = an installment payment.  "cycle" = one recurring charge.
  "SF" = Salesforce.  "QB" = QuickBooks Online.

2. SYSTEM OVERVIEW (THE BUSINESS)
================================================================================
The company enrolls people (Leads/Candidates) into paid programs. It bills them in
Salesforce, pushes the invoice to QuickBooks, and collects money either as a single
one-time payment (possibly in several partials) or as an automatic recurring
installment plan (EMIs). Everything the customer pays in QuickBooks flows back into
Salesforce as Payment records, and the Invoice keeps running totals and status.

Two fundamental invoice shapes (Invoice__c.Type__c):

  A) ONE-TIME INVOICE  (Type__c = "One-Time")
     - A single "upfront amount" invoice created from a LEAD or an ACCOUNT via a
       screen flow ("Send Invoice" button).
     - The customer can pay it all at once, or in several partial payments until it
       is fully paid.

  B) RECURRING PLAN  (Type__c = "Recurring")
     - An installment/subscription plan billed automatically by a QuickBooks
       "Recurring Sales Receipt" (e.g. 10 monthly EMIs of a fixed amount).
     - Each automatic charge creates a Payment record = one "cycle".
     - Sized by an ACH/salary calculator (Total Salary x Agreement %), optionally
       with interest, and managed by the "Manage Recurring Plan" flow.

  LINKED ONE-TIME INVOICES (a one-time invoice attached to a recurring plan):
     A One-Time invoice can be linked to a plan (Linked_Recurring_Plan__c) as:
       * "Pre EMI"  - candidate prepaid one or more EMIs through a separate invoice.
       * "Lump Sum" - candidate paid a large one-off amount to reduce the balance.
       * "Recovery" - an invoice raised to re-collect money lost to a void/dispute.
     Pre-EMI and Lump-Sum both reduce the plan's remaining balance; Recovery re-collects
     money already lost.

3. INTEGRATION ARCHITECTURE (SF <-> QUICKBOOKS)
================================================================================
- NAMED CREDENTIAL: "QuickBooks" (OAuth 2.0 to QuickBooks Online, production company).
- COMPANY ID: Custom Label QB_Company_ID (passed as {CompanyID} in every endpoint).
- EXTERNAL SERVICES: QuickBooks REST endpoints are registered as OpenAPI schemas
  ("External Service Registrations"). Salesforce generates strongly-typed Apex request/
  response classes from each schema, and Flows call them as "Actions". Changing an
  endpoint's fields means editing the registration's schema and redeploying (which
  regenerates the Apex types). See section 19 for the endpoint list.
- OUTBOUND (SF -> QB): create/update customer, create/update invoice, create recurring
  sales receipt, update recurring transaction, send invoice email, manage bank account,
  get invoice / recurring / sales receipt / deposit / cards / bank accounts.
- INBOUND WEBHOOKS (QB -> SF): QuickBooks posts entity events; record-triggered/
  autolaunched Flows process them:
      Payment      (Create/Update/Void) -> QB_Process_Payment_Webhook       (one-time payments)
      SalesReceipt (Create/Update/Void) -> QB_Process_SalesReceipt_Webhook   (recurring cycles)
      Deposit      (Create/Update)      -> updates Payment deposit fields
- CRITICAL QB LIMITATION: QuickBooks does NOT send a webhook for an ACH return, a
  dispute, or a chargeback. The SalesReceipt/Payment entity is not modified when a
  dispute happens. These are detected via Intuit notification EMAILS and/or by polling,
  and can also be logged manually by Finance.

5. DATA MODEL & OBJECT RELATIONSHIP
================================================================================
        Invoice__c  (1) -------< Payment__c  (many)
                     ^ lookup: Payment__c.Invoice__c

- One Invoice has many Payments. Roll-up summary fields on Invoice__c aggregate the
  child Payments (Amount_Paid, Total_Amount_Collected, Total_Cycles_Completed,
  Voided_Amount, Voided_Payment_Count).
- A RECURRING plan's Payments are its EMI cycles: Payment_Source = "Sales Receipt",
  and each carries a QB_Sales_Receipt_ID__c.
- A ONE-TIME invoice's Payments are direct invoice payments: Payment_Source =
  "Invoice Payment", no QB_Sales_Receipt_ID.
- Invoice__c also self-references via Linked_Recurring_Plan__c (a linked one-time
  invoice points to the recurring-plan invoice it reduces) and Settlement_Invoice__c.

7. INVOICE__C - EXACT FORMULAS & ROLL-UPS  (verbatim logic)
================================================================================
FORMULA FIELDS:
  ACH_Amount__c        = Total_Salary__c * BLANKVALUE(Agreement_Percent__c, 0)
  Base_Amount__c       = Invoice_Amount__c - BLANKVALUE(Card_Surcharge_Amount__c, 0)
  Is_Recurring__c      = ISPICKVAL(Type__c, "Recurring")
  Total_Salary__c      = BLANKVALUE(Annual_Salary__c, 0) + BLANKVALUE(Bonus_Amount__c, 0)

  Collected_So_Far__c  = BLANKVALUE(Total_Amount_Collected__c,0) + BLANKVALUE(Total_Lumpsum_Amount__c,0)
                         + BLANKVALUE(Total_Prepaid_Amount__c,0) + BLANKVALUE(Settlement_Amount__c,0)

  Collected_Percent__c = IF(Total_To_Collect__c > 0,
                             (Collected_So_Far__c - BLANKVALUE(Amount_To_Recover__c,0)) / Total_To_Collect__c,
                             0)

  Outstanding_Cycles__c = IF(ISBLANK(Number_of_Months__c), null,
                             Number_of_Months__c - Total_Cycles_Completed__c)

  Outstanding_Amount__c =
     IF( ISPICKVAL(Recurring_Status__c,"Settled"), 0,
       IF( ISPICKVAL(Type__c,"Recurring"),
           IF( Agreed_Plan_Total__c > 0,
               (Agreed_Plan_Total__c + BLANKVALUE(Plan_Interest_Amount__c,0)) - Total_Amount_Collected__c,
               IF( ISBLANK(Number_of_Months__c), null,
                   ((Recurring_Amount__c * Number_of_Months__c) + BLANKVALUE(Plan_Interest_Amount__c,0))
                    - Total_Amount_Collected__c ) ),
           Invoice_Amount__c - Amount_Paid__c ) )

  Total_Plan_Value__c =
     IF( ISPICKVAL(Type__c,"Recurring"),
         IF( Agreed_Plan_Total__c > 0,
             Agreed_Plan_Total__c + BLANKVALUE(Plan_Interest_Amount__c,0),
             IF( ISBLANK(Number_of_Months__c), null,
                 (Recurring_Amount__c * Number_of_Months__c) + BLANKVALUE(Plan_Interest_Amount__c,0) ) ),
         Invoice_Amount__c )

  Total_To_Collect__c =
     IF( ACH_Amount__c > 0,
         ACH_Amount__c + IF(Charge_Interest__c,
             IF(Committed_Interest__c > 0, Committed_Interest__c, BLANKVALUE(Plan_Interest_Amount__c,0)), 0),
         BLANKVALUE(Total_Plan_Value__c, BLANKVALUE(Invoice_Amount__c,0)) )

  Interest_Amount__c =
     IF( OR(NOT(Is_Recurring__c), NOT(Charge_Interest__c)), 0,
         IF( Committed_Interest__c > 0, Committed_Interest__c,
             BLANKVALUE(ACH_Amount__c,0) * BLANKVALUE(Interest_Rate_Per_Cycle__c,0) * BLANKVALUE(Number_of_Months__c,0) ) )

  Interest_Per_Cycle_Base__c =
     IF( AND(Is_Recurring__c, Charge_Interest__c, Outstanding_Cycles__c > 0),
         MAX( 0,
              IF( Committed_Interest__c > 0, Committed_Interest__c,
                  IF( Plan_Interest_Amount__c > 0, Plan_Interest_Amount__c,
                      BLANKVALUE(ACH_Amount__c,0) * BLANKVALUE(Interest_Rate_Per_Cycle__c,0) * BLANKVALUE(Number_of_Months__c,0) ) )
              - BLANKVALUE(Interest_Collected__c,0) ) / Outstanding_Cycles__c,
         0 )

  QB_Customer_Id__c =
     IF( NOT(ISBLANK(Lead__r.QB_Customer_ID__c)), Lead__r.QB_Customer_ID__c,
         IF( NOT(ISBLANK(Candidate__r.QB_Customer_ID__c)), Candidate__r.QB_Customer_ID__c, "" ) )

  Schedule_Description__c =
     "Every " & IF(Interval__c=1, "", TEXT(Interval__c) & " ") &
     CASE( TEXT(Frequency__c), "Daily", IF(Interval__c=1,"day","days"),
           "Weekly", IF(Interval__c=1,"week","weeks"),
           "Monthly", IF(Interval__c=1,"month","months"),
           "Yearly", IF(Interval__c=1,"year","years"), "" )

  Invoice_Link__c  = HYPERLINK($Label.QB_Admin_Base_URL & QB_Invoice_Id__c,
                               $Label.QB_Admin_Base_URL & QB_Invoice_Id__c, "_blank")
  Payment_Link__c  = HYPERLINK($Label.QB_Payment_Base_URL & QB_Invoice_Id__c,
                               $Label.QB_Payment_Base_URL & QB_Invoice_Id__c, "_blank")

ROLL-UP SUMMARY FIELDS (child = Payment__c, key = Payment__c.Invoice__c):
  Amount_Paid__c            = SUM(Payment.Amount_Paid)    WHERE Payment.Payment_Status = "Paid"
  Total_Amount_Collected__c = SUM(Payment.Payment_Amount) (no filter - includes voided)
  Total_Cycles_Completed__c = COUNT(Payment)              WHERE Payment.QB_Sales_Receipt_ID <> blank
  Voided_Amount__c          = SUM(Payment.Payment_Amount) WHERE Payment.Payment_Status = "Voided"
  Voided_Payment_Count__c   = COUNT(Payment)              WHERE Payment.Payment_Status = "Voided"

11. CORE BUSINESS RULES & LOGIC
================================================================================
11.1 INVOICE STATUS (one-time invoices) is CUMULATIVE
     cumulative_paid = 0                     -> "Not Paid"
     0 < cumulative_paid < Invoice_Amount     -> "Partially Paid"
     cumulative_paid >= Invoice_Amount        -> "Paid"
     Several partial payments that add up to the total MUST flip the invoice to "Paid"
     on the completing payment. (See section 14 for the exact webhook calculation.)

11.2 INTEREST is RECURRING-ONLY
     Interest_Amount and Interest_Per_Cycle_Base are 0 unless Is_Recurring AND Charge_Interest.
     On a one-time invoice, Interest_Rate_Per_Cycle (default 2%) has no effect.

11.3 TOTAL_CYCLES_COMPLETED counts ONLY recurring cycles
     Roll-up COUNT of Payments filtered to QB_Sales_Receipt_ID <> blank. A one-time invoice
     paid in N partials shows 0 cycles.

11.4 COLLECTED % nets out recoverable money
     Collected_Percent = (Collected_So_Far - Amount_To_Recover) / Total_To_Collect.

11.5 AMOUNT PAID vs TOTAL AMOUNT COLLECTED
     Amount_Paid excludes voided payments (Status=Paid filter); Total_Amount_Collected
     includes everything. Outstanding (one-time) uses Amount_Paid; Outstanding (recurring)
     uses Total_Amount_Collected.

11.6 OUTSTANDING differs by type (see exact formula in section 7):
     One-Time : Invoice_Amount - Amount_Paid.
     Recurring: (principal + plan interest) - Total_Amount_Collected; 0 if Settled.

12. THE RECURRING FIXED-EMI AMORTIZATION MODEL (DEEP DIVE)
================================================================================
The plan keeps a FIXED EMI (Recurring_Amount). When the plan changes (reduce, lump sum,
change interest, add/remove linked invoices), the NUMBER OF CYCLES is recomputed, not the EMI.

  committed_interest = IF Charge_Interest: (Committed_Interest, else Plan_Interest_Amount) else 0
  remaining = (ACH_Amount + committed_interest)
              - Total_Amount_Collected - Total_Prepaid_Amount - Total_Lumpsum_Amount
  full_cycles = FLOOR( ROUND(remaining,2) / EMI )
  remainder   = ROUND(remaining,2) - full_cycles*EMI
  total_remaining_cycles = full_cycles + (1 if remainder > 0.005 else 0)
  Last_EMI_Amount = (remainder > 0.005) ? remainder : 0     <- smaller final payment
  Number_of_Months = Total_Cycles_Completed + total_remaining_cycles
  QB MaxOccurrences pushed = Number_of_Months - Total_Cycles_Completed  (remaining charges only)

- Pre-EMI and Lump-Sum both subtract their Base_Amount from "remaining" (same schedule effect);
  the difference is only which bucket they report in (Total_Prepaid vs Total_Lumpsum).
- Reschedule is the exception: it RE-AMORTIZES the remaining balance over a chosen new number
  of months, so the EMI changes: new EMI = (total_with_interest - collected) / remaining_cycles.
- Every QB recurring update first GETs the recurring transaction (fresh SyncToken) before POSTing.

13. MANAGE RECURRING PLAN - EVERY ACTION
================================================================================
The QB_Manage_Recurring_Plan screen flow offers (menu adapts to plan state):
  Skip Next Charge      Push the next charge by one cycle (schedule start moves), same total count.
  Reduce Cycles         Candidate prepaid an EMI: reduce remaining count by 1, EMI unchanged.
  Reschedule Length     Change total months; EMI re-amortized over the remaining balance.
  Change Amount         Re-derive the plan from a new salary/agreement (recompute EMI/total).
  Change Interest       Adjust interest; EMI fixed, cycle count recomputes.
  Change Frequency      Daily/Weekly/Monthly + interval; billing day derived from a start date.
  Change Billing Date   Pick a date; billing day is derived automatically.
  Apply Lump Sum        Apply a large one-off (from a paid one-time invoice); reduce balance.
  Extend                Change the total number of payments (re-amortize over new term).
  Change Plan / Item    Switch the QuickBooks product/item; charge amount stays the same.
  Change Billing Email  Update the QB receipt email.
  Pause / Resume        Temporarily stop / restart billing.
  Settle / Early Payoff Close the plan now (remaining + Amount_To_Recover, minus discount).
  Manage Linked Invoices View / Add / Remove linked invoices; Change type (Pre-EMI <-> Lump Sum).
  View in QuickBooks    Just shows where the template lives (no change).
- Every action increments Manage_Change_Count (so the Slack notify flow always fires),
  stamps Last_Recurring_Action, updates the SF Invoice, and syncs the new schedule/
  MaxOccurrences to QuickBooks (with a fresh SyncToken).
- The menu is gated: brand-new plans (0 cycles) show setup-style options; mid-plan plans
  (>0 cycles) show Skip/Reduce/Lump/Change Interest/Manage Links/Settle; paused plans show
  Resume/View only.

14. PAYMENT WEBHOOK LOGIC (status & cycles)
================================================================================
QB_Process_Payment_Webhook handles QB Payment Create / Update / Void for ONE-TIME invoices.
  Amountpaid (this payment) = QB Payment.TotalAmt - Payment.UnappliedAmt
  Invoice status is set from the CUMULATIVE paid amount:
    - CREATE (new payment): cumulative = Invoice.Amount_Paid (read before the new payment
      commits, so = prior payments) + Amountpaid.
    - UPDATE (existing payment changed): cumulative = Invoice.Amount_Paid - this payment's
      OLD Amount_Paid + Amountpaid.
    status = (cumulative=0)?"Not Paid" : (cumulative < Invoice_Amount)?"Partially Paid":"Paid"
  Payment_Type = (status="Paid")?"Full":"Partial".
  VOID: Payment_Status="Voided", Transaction_Status="Cancelled", Amount_Paid=0, Invoice status "Void".

QB_Process_SalesReceipt_Webhook handles recurring EMI charges:
  - Creates a Payment with Payment_Source="Sales Receipt" and QB_Sales_Receipt_ID set
    (this is what advances Total_Cycles_Completed).
  - Marks the plan Completed once the last cycle is billed
    ( (Total_Cycles_Completed + 1) >= Number_of_Months ).

15. DISPUTES, VOIDS, RECOVERY & SETTLEMENT
================================================================================
DETECTION: QuickBooks does NOT webhook disputes/ACH-returns/chargebacks. They are found via
Intuit notification emails (3 types: Dispute / ACH Return / Bank Transfer Canceled) matched to
a Payment by QB_Doc_Number, and/or logged manually by Finance (QB_Log_Dispute_Manually, which
sets Dispute_Source = "Manual - Finance"; automatic detection sets "Auto"). CC-card chargebacks
are invisible in the API and are email-only.

WHERE THE RECOVERABLE AMOUNT LANDS (Amount_To_Recover):
  - Recurring sales-receipt dispute    -> add to the PLAN's Amount_To_Recover.
  - Linked one-time (Pre-EMI/Lump)     -> recover to the PLAN.
  - Standalone one-time                -> recover to ITSELF.
  - One-time invoice with several payments where one is disputed -> Invoice status
    "Partial Dispute"; recover the disputed payment's amount.

DISPUTE OUTCOMES (Payment_Issue_Outcome):
  Won         -> no recovery needed.
  Lost        -> money lost; recover it.
  Refunded    -> refunded; recover it.
  Re-collected-> already re-collected.
  Written Off -> written off (still counts as needing recovery per business rule).
  A "Recovery" linked invoice (Linked_Payment_Type="Recovery") is raised to re-collect;
  when it is paid, Amount_To_Recover goes down. Recovery invoices are EXCLUDED from the
  candidate's "collected" totals to avoid double counting.

SETTLEMENT / EARLY PAYOFF:
  Creates a settlement invoice for (remaining balance + Amount_To_Recover), optionally with a
  discount and/or 4% card surcharge; sets Recurring_Status=Settled, zeroes the recurring
  amount and Amount_To_Recover, and cancels the QuickBooks recurring transaction.


================================================================================
16. CARD SURCHARGE (4%) RULES
================================================================================
- If the customer pays by credit card, a 4% surcharge is added:
    Invoice_Amount = Base_Amount x 1.04 ;  Card_Surcharge_Amount = Base_Amount x 0.04 ;
    Card_Surcharge_Applied = TRUE.
- The single QB invoice line's Amount is the surcharged total (Qty 1 x UnitPrice).
- Editing an invoice can toggle the surcharge off, dropping Invoice_Amount back to Base_Amount.
- On a settlement, a 4% surcharge can likewise be added if the payoff is by card.

17. QUICKBOOKS INVOICE LINE STRUCTURE
================================================================================
Each invoice/sales-receipt line SF builds contains:
  DetailType = "SalesItemLineDetail"
  Amount     = line amount (upfront amount, or EMI)
  Description= human label, e.g. "Upfront Amount"     <- set on BOTH create AND edit
  SalesItemLineDetail.ItemRef.name  = the Plan name
  SalesItemLineDetail.ItemRef.value = the QuickBooks Item Number (from QB_Plan setting)
  SalesItemLineDetail.Qty           = 1
  SalesItemLineDetail.UnitPrice     = the rate (= Amount for Qty 1)
Note: a QuickBooks SPARSE update only sends provided fields, so the edit flow must resend
Description + Qty + Rate or they get wiped. (The SaveInvoice External Service schema was
extended to include Qty and UnitPrice so editing preserves them.)

20. WORKED EXAMPLES (END TO END)
================================================================================
EXAMPLE A - one-time invoice paid in partials (real: INV-0199 from a Lead):
  Base 2,000, surcharge OFF -> Invoice_Amount 2,000, Outstanding 2,000, "Not Paid".
  Payment 1 = 800  (Partial) -> Amount_Paid 800,  Outstanding 1,200, Collected% 40%, "Partially Paid".
  Payment 2 = 1,200 (Full)   -> Amount_Paid 2,000, Outstanding 0,     Collected% 100%, "Paid".
  Total_Cycles_Completed stays 0 (Invoice Payments, no Sales Receipt Id).
  Invoice_Link/Payment_Link resolve to the QB invoice via QB_Invoice_Id.

EXAMPLE B - a recurring plan cycle:
  A monthly Recurring Sales Receipt fires in QB -> SalesReceipt webhook -> creates a Payment
  (Payment_Source "Sales Receipt", QB_Sales_Receipt_ID set) -> Total_Cycles_Completed +1,
  Total_Amount_Collected +EMI, Outstanding recomputes; when the last cycle bills,
  Recurring_Status -> Completed.

EXAMPLE C - lump sum on a plan:
  CS runs Manage Recurring Plan -> Apply Lump Sum, selecting a paid one-time invoice; that
  invoice is linked (Linked_Payment_Type "Lump Sum", Linked_Recurring_Plan = the plan);
  Total_Lumpsum_Amount += its Base_Amount; the plan re-amortizes (fewer cycles, same EMI,
  smaller Last_EMI), and QB MaxOccurrences is updated.

21. KNOWN GOTCHAS & PRODUCTION-MIGRATION NOTES
================================================================================
- SANDBOX vs PROD config to swap at go-live: QB_Undeposited_Funds_Account_ID (4 -> prod value),
  QB_ACH_Payment_Method_ID, QuickBooks Item Numbers in the QB_Plan setting, and
  QB_Test_Active_Plan = true -> false.
- Interest_Rate_Per_Cycle is a Percent input that defaults to 2; a stray value on a one-time
  invoice is cosmetic only (interest formulas are gated to recurring).
- A QuickBooks SPARSE update wipes any line field you don't resend - always resend
  Description + Qty + UnitPrice on edit.
- Every recurring update must GET the recurring transaction first for a fresh SyncToken,
  or the POST fails.
- Roll-up filter changes (e.g. Total_Cycles_Completed) recalculate asynchronously across
  all records after deploy.
- Invoice status only re-evaluates on a payment webhook; an already-fully-paid invoice that
  predates a logic fix will not self-correct until the next payment event (fix manually).

22. GLOSSARY
================================================================================
ACH             Bank transfer / eCheck. Recurring plans usually bill by ACH.
EMI / cycle     One installment / one recurring charge.
Upfront Amount  The one-time amount billed on a Lead/Account invoice.
Pre-EMI         A separate invoice where the candidate prepaid one or more EMIs.
Lump Sum        A large one-off payment that reduces a plan's balance.
Recovery        An invoice raised to re-collect money lost to a void/dispute.
Settlement      Early payoff that closes a plan (remaining + recovery, minus discount).
Sparse update   A QB update that sends only changed fields (sparse=true).
SyncToken       QB optimistic-lock token; must be current on every update.
Committed Interest  Plan interest locked at first payment so it never compounds on re-amortization.
Last EMI        The smaller final remainder charged on the last cycle (EMI stays fixed).
Amount To Recover   Money lost to voids/disputes that still needs to be re-collected.
Collected %     (Collected_So_Far - Amount_To_Recover) / Total_To_Collect.
