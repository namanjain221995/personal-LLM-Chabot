# vLLM upgrade research — closing the GDN "misaligned address" fault class on the DGX Spark pair

Workstream: research (read-only). No file in the worktree was created or edited. All findings below are from upstream sources fetched 2026-09-12 (GitHub API/pages, Docker Hub API, docs.vllm.ai, vllm.ai blog, NVIDIA playbook, the HF model card as shipped in the local checkpoint) plus read-only inspection of the running head container (`docker inspect`, `docker logs`, `docker exec … cat` of installed vLLM source) and a local diff of the installed GDN sources against `v0.29.0`.

## Executive summary

1. **No released or nightly vLLM build contains a fix for this fault class, and the two PRs the 2026-09-03 diagnosis relied on do not apply.** `#51812` ("Align Qwen GDN gates with speculative tokens", merged 2026-08-11) is a correctness fix for the spec-decode gate ordering; `#51674` (fused CUDA post-conv **MTP decode** kernel, merged 2026-08-14) explicitly "does NOT touch fused_gdn_prefill_post_conv.py, the prefill path, or mixed prefill+decode batches". `fused_gdn_prefill_post_conv.py` has had **zero commits** since it was moved on 2026-07-16 (#48500). I diffed the installed `qwen_gdn_linear_attn.py`, `gdn_attn.py` and `causal_conv1d.py` against `v0.29.0`: the non-speculative mixed prefill+decode path (`split_non_spec` → `causal_conv1d_fn` → `fused_post_conv_prep` → `chunk_gated_delta_rule`) is functionally unchanged; the only diffs are MTP/spec-decode work, refactors, and PDL launch flags added to `causal_conv1d` by the Kimi-K3 PRs.
2. **Upstream has two open tracking issues that match our incident exactly and reproduce on every build from 0.23.1 to 0.28.1rc1 (MRV2), including a two-DGX-Spark TP=2 pair on 2026-09-07.** `#49926` ("EngineDeadError NVFP4 marlin", open since 2026-07-27, no maintainer reply): same model (`nvidia/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4`), same GPU (GB10 sm_121), same Xid 13 class 0xcec0 + Xid 31 MMU fault @0x0 signature, TP=1, mixed chunked-prefill-tail + decodes. `#37431` (open since 2026-03-18, 17 comments, no label, no assignee) is the umbrella thread for GDN/Mamba Triton faults and silent wedges on sm_121; its refuted-mitigation ledger includes: MTP off, `PIECEWISE`, newer nightlies, `max_num_seqs=1` (which refutes "mixed batch is required"), capped capture sizes, `cudagraph_mode: NONE` (faulted the next day under mixed long prefill + decode), and backports of #55506 / #50729. The sibling on sm_120 (`#54331`) shows the same class on 0.26.0 → 0.28.0 with v0.24.0 clean; only `--enforce-eager` survived a five-arm dissection.
3. **The faulting kernel is not proven to be GDN.** Every traceback in the thread (ours included) is an asynchronous sticky-error observation point; reports land in `fused_post_conv_prep`, `initial_state[~mask] = 0`, `async_copy_ready_event.synchronize()`, `cudaGraphLaunch`, and cuBLAS. Two structural pointers exist: (a) a non-GDN NVFP4 MoE (Gemma-4-26B) on the same GB10, same Marlin backend, same fp8 KV, ran 24 days clean — pointing at the GDN Triton family; (b) two users report weeks without a crash after switching the MoE backend from Marlin to `flashinfer_b12x` — pointing at the Marlin W4A16 MoE path. Neither has been confirmed by a maintainer.
4. **Recommendation: do not change the production image as part of this programme. Stay pinned on `sha256:24f2f897…`, rely on the engine controller + worker sentinel + orchestrator fallback (CONTRACT §6/§8) to bound the damage, and run two bounded experiments in a change window with the soak as the gate** — Track A first (`--moe-backend flashinfer_b12x` on the *current* build; no pull; reversible in one manifest line), Track B second (`nightly-385dce36…` of 2026-09-09, the first build carrying #55715 "FlashInfer GDN prefill kernel on SM12x", pinned by digest). `v0.29.0` itself is **not** a candidate: it ships Model Runner V2 as default *without* the #55341 workspace-lock fix (an IMA-in-graph-replay bug, #55336), it does not contain #55715, and the 1.8× long-prefill regression (#53787) that opened after the v0.28.0 branch point is unbisected and likely inside it.
5. **Timeouts / fault tolerance:** `VLLM_EXECUTE_MODEL_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` (default 300, "only applies when TP > 1") is unchanged in v0.29.0 and remains the *only* engine-side bound on a dead remote rank. The `vllm/v1/fault_tolerance/engine_core_sentinel.py` module is byte-for-byte the same framework in both builds: reactive (it runs only after the busy loop raises, i.e. after the 300 s), and `--enable-fault-tolerance` is rejected unless `--data-parallel-external-lb`/`--data-parallel-rank` is set. It cannot be enabled for our TP=2 single-engine deployment and would not detect a dead rank faster if it could. The head's `MultiprocWorkerMonitor` watches local child processes only; there is no cross-node liveness signal in any version. The CONTRACT §6.4 sentinel is the correct fix and has no upstream substitute.

### 1.3 The upstream evidence that this class is open and version-independent

- [#49926](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/49926) "[Bug]: EngineDeadError NVFP4 marlin" — opened 2026-07-27, **open**, 6 comments, no maintainer reply. GB10 sm_121, DGX Spark, `nvidia/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4`, TP=1, `modelopt_mixed`, `kv_cache_dtype=fp8`, Marlin MoE, FlashInfer attention. Xid 13 "Graphics Exception: channel 0x2, Class 0000cec0" + Xid 31 "MMU Fault … faulted @ 0x0_00000000 … FAULT_PTE" (Family A) and Xid 13 "Out Of Range Address" grid-wide + Xid 43 under graph replay (Family B). Batch at fault: "one chunked-prefill tail + 21 single-token decodes". Reproduced on 0.25.1, 0.26.0 (reporter), 0.23.1rc1.dev1279 nightly (2026-07-20 image, comment 2026-07-28 — a *pure single-token decode step*, `num_running_reqs=1`), 0.25.1.dev24 and 0.26.1rc1.dev191 (comment 2026-08-06 — one crash surfaced at `qwen_gdn_linear_attn.py:1434 initial_state[~prefill_has_initial_state, ...] = 0`, with and without MTP, `VLLM_MARLIN_USE_ATOMIC_ADD=0` live). This is our Xid pair (remediation §1.1: "Xid 13 (Graphics Exception, class 0xcec0) and Xid 31 (MMU fault, GPC2, @ 0x0)").
- [#37431](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/37431) "Mamba-2 Triton kernels crash with illegal instruction on SM121 (DGX Spark) without CUDA_LAUNCH_BLOCKING=1" — opened 2026-03-18, **open**, 17 comments, no label, no assignee. Datapoints relevant to us:
  - 2026-08-04/05 (vlnofka, 0.26.0, TP=1, prefix caching **off**, `--no-async-scheduling`): "six silent hangs and two illegal memory access crashes" in 9 days; py-spy with `CUDA_LAUNCH_BLOCKING=1` parked *inside* `cudaGraphLaunch` on a **pure-decode batch of 4**; `PIECEWISE` "does not fix it" (died after 9 h 57 m at a **decode batch of 2**, KV 2.2 %).
  - 2026-08-06 (flerbert12983, 0.25.1.dev24): three silent wedges in 6 days — "GPU util pinned at 96 % while drawing only 4–17 W, API port open, `/v1/models` 200" — py-spy at `qwen_gdn_linear_attn.py:1572` right after the `chunk_gated_delta_rule(...)` launch (this is the signature in our `vllm-head-wedges-while-healthy` note).
  - 2026-08-14 (andrei-dotdna, 21-day 2×2 on four boxes): 7 failures 61–72 h apart; ledger of six mitigations that did **not** work (MTP off; `PIECEWISE` → "converted the hang into a hard Xid-13 crash"; newer nightly; removing multimodal traffic; **`max_num_seqs=1` — "soft-locked anyway … refutes the mixed prefill+decode hypothesis"**; `cudagraph_capture_sizes=[1,2,4]`). Control: Gemma-4-26B-A4B-NVFP4 on the **same GB10, same image, same Marlin NVFP4 MoE, same fp8 KV** — 24 days clean. Also: "`--gdn-prefill-backend flashinfer|cutedsl` parses but does nothing" on GB10 "through FlashInfer 0.6.18 … That single build change would move GB10 off the implicated kernel entirely" (this is what #55715 later did).
  - **2026-09-07 (sequarell, two DGX Sparks, TP=2 over RoCE, `0.28.1rc1.dev393+ga5c9179e7`, Model Runner V2, MTP=3, prefix caching on):** both ranks Xid 31 (head FAULT_PDE read, worker FAULT_PTE write, 10 s apart) at `num_running_reqs=3`, KV 10 %. Refuted: backport of #55506 and presence of #50729. `cudagraph_mode: NONE` ran 10 h 46 m then **faulted the next day** "~185 s after" a load of "mixed long prefill against running decode … cold long prefills overlapping with in-flight decodes" (our soak's shape). A `mode 0 + NONE` (compile off) arm passed only a 497 s test; explicitly "not yet a causal result".
- [#54331](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/54331) (sm_120 sibling, opened 2026-08-29, open, 0 comments): on RTX PRO 6000, `ornith-ai/Ornith-1.5-35B-A3B-NVFP4` and `nvidia/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-NVFP4` die in ~7–8 min under saturation "whenever CUDA graphs are on — persists 0.26.0 → 0.27.0 → 0.27.1 → 0.28.0, clean on 0.24.0"; five-arm dissection: FULL/PIECEWISE, `TRITON_ATTN`, `expandable_segments` all crash; **only `enforce_eager` survives**. (Note: on sm_121 the class predates 0.24 — #49926's 0.23.1 datapoint — so the sm_120 "regression window" does not transfer.)
- [#52225](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/52225) (v0.27.1, sm_120, Nemotron-3.5-Lightning NVFP4 hybrid Mamba-2, FlashInfer, fp8 KV, Marlin): recurring Xid 13 Misaligned Address / Illegal Instruction / Out-Of-Range-Register bursts under 5–10 concurrent multi-turn streams, 1–20+ h MTBF; open, no fix.
- [#52540](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/52540) (v0.26.0/v0.27.1, sm_120, Qwen3.8-27B ModelOpt mixed, FlashInfer CUTLASS NVFP4 linear): IMA after 16–21 min; workaround `VLLM_DISABLED_KERNELS=FlashInferCutlassNvFp4LinearKernel`. Not our kernel (GB10 uses Marlin for the NVFP4 linears) but the same "NVFP4 + hybrid GDN on Blackwell consumer/SoC" cluster.
- [#50934](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/50934) (GB10, Nemotron-3-Super NVFP4, Marlin, MTP): misaligned address after ~10 days at a single mid-decode request; open.
- Ruled out as a cause: [#56064](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/56064) (2026-09-09, GB10 `moe_wna16_marlin_gemm` IMA at M=256) — the reporter and reviewer agree it is reachable only with a patched no-split-K partitioning: "the unmodified op cannot reach the state".

**Conclusion:** the fault class is open upstream, has no maintainer diagnosis, reproduces on TP=1 and on a two-Spark TP=2 pair, with and without MTP, with and without prefix caching, on pure-decode and on mixed batches, on every build from 2026-07 to 2026-09. Any upgrade is an experiment, not a fix.

NVIDIA's own DGX Spark playbook (last updated 2026-06-12) points two-Spark users at `nvcr.io/nvidia/vllm:26.05-py3` with Ray — an *older* vLLM line than ours, not a candidate; the vLLM blog "vLLM on the DGX Spark" (2026-06-01) recommends the CUDA-13 nightly track but says to "validate the model recipe against a specific release image, commit-specific nightly tag, or image digest, then keep that exact image reference in your runbook." The HF model card's DGX Spark recipe uses `vllm/vllm-openai:nightly`, `--moe-backend marlin`, `--attention-backend flashinfer`, `--kv-cache-dtype fp8` (our configuration).

## 5. Timeouts and dead-rank detection in the candidates

- `VLLM_EXECUTE_MODEL_TIMEOUT_SECONDS`: same name, same default **300**, same two call sites (`execute_model`, `sample_tokens` in `vllm/v1/executor/multiproc_executor.py`) in the running build and in `v0.29.0`. It is the only bound on a wedged remote rank. `--distributed-timeout-seconds` is the torch.distributed init/collective timeout, not the step clock (as the remediation record already corrected). `VLLM_ENGINE_ITERATION_TIMEOUT_S=60` is defined but unused by V1 code. `VLLM_WORKER_SHUTDOWN_TIMEOUT_SECONDS=5` bounds local worker shutdown, not the `vllm serve` teardown hang we saw (the Docker healthcheck's 4 × 30 s remains the bound there; the CONTRACT's controller restarts it earlier).
- Dead-rank detection: `MultiprocExecutor.start_worker_monitor` waits on `multiprocessing.connection.wait(sentinels)` of **local child processes only** (identical in v0.29.0); the worker node's own headless executor is what logged "Worker proc … died unexpectedly" at 22:20:41 after apport released the process. There is no cross-node heartbeat or death signal in any version; the head learns of a remote rank's death only through the 300 s RPC timeout ("No available shared memory broadcast block found in 60 seconds" is a *log message* from `shm_broadcast.py:104`, not a timeout).
- `vllm/v1/fault_tolerance/engine_core_sentinel.py` (present in both builds, identical shape): `EngineCoreSentinel.on_fault()` is invoked by `fault_tolerant_wrapper` only *after* the busy loop raises; it marks the engine `UNHEALTHY`/`DEAD`, aborts requests, pushes a status to the client, and waits `engine_recovery_timeout_sec` (default 120) for a `retry` instruction (`POST /fault_tolerance/apply`, `GET /fault_tolerance/status`), whose only implemented instruction re-inits the **DP** process group. Enablement is `--enable-fault-tolerance` and it raises "Fault tolerance requires external load balancer mode (`--data-parallel-external-lb` or `--data-parallel-rank`). Internal LB mode is not supported." (`arg_utils.py`, both builds) and "requires a single API server process". It is designed for scaling down a faulted DP engine in DP+EP deployments (v0.27.0 notes: "a (simplified) fault tolerance framework for DP+EP external load-balancer deployments"); it cannot be enabled for TP=2/DP=1 and would not shorten detection if it could. **No per-step watchdog or engine-level liveness probe for remote TP ranks exists in v0.29.0 or main.** The CONTRACT §6.3/§6.4 design (sentinel on the worker, real-completion canary on the head) is the right response and has no upstream equivalent to wait for. On lowering the 300 s clock: still a judgement call (remediation §11 kept it for the JIT-stall reason); with the sentinel in place the controller will act in seconds, so the engine clock only matters as a last-resort bound — leave at 300 unless the JIT-stall measurement lands.

## 6. Recommendation, rollback, validation plan

### 6.1 Recommendation

**No candidate is clearly safe. Stay pinned on `sha256:24f2f8975…` for production and let the availability programme (controller + sentinel + `core: 1` + breaker + fallback) bound the damage.** Rationale: the fault class has no upstream fix, reproduces on the newest builds including MRV2 on a two-Spark TP=2 pair, and every candidate adds unrelated risk (MRV2 switch, an un-cherry-picked MRV2 IMA fix in v0.29.0, an unbisected 1.8× long-prefill regression in everything after v0.28.0, new PDL kernels, new all-reduce default). Do **not** recommend `latest`/`nightly` floating tags.

Run two bounded experiments in a scheduled change window, each gated by §6.4, in this order:

- **Track A — `--moe-backend flashinfer_b12x` on the current pinned build.** No pull, no image change; a one-line change to `config/model-manifest.yaml:210` `startup_arguments` (rendered into `MAIN_STARTUP_ARGUMENTS` and `CLUSTER_ENGINE_ARGS`, byte-identical on both ranks via `scripts/cluster-sync.sh --env-only`), reversible in ~6 min via `./techsara up`. Evidence for: the only positive stability reports against this class on GB10 with this model (bvowell1975 2026-08-07 on 0.26.0; andrei-dotdna adopted on 0.27.1 2026-08-14 with the explicit caveat "not yet evidence"). Evidence against / risks: vLLM's own "excluded from auto-selection until the upstream CUTLASS SM121 MMA op guard is resolved"; #47365 (TP=2 garbage output on SM120 — bisected to a modelopt rule that resolves correctly for our checkpoint revision, but output must be checked, not assumed); #56535 (W4A16 checkpoint executed as W4A4 — quality must be measured); ~+5 GiB/rank weights (extrapolated from +10.7 GiB at TP=1) on a head with 21–30 GiB MemAvailable; long noisy warm-up; #50189 (b12x Xid 31 under heavy concurrent chunked prefill on SM120). Gate: §6.4 plus an output-quality check (identical-prompt greedy comparison vs Marlin on 20 prompts, tool-call and JSON-schema samples, needles 3/3 at 8K/32K/131K).
- **Track B — `nightly-385dce36bcee42309924a5ece951a96db3dce7f2` (2026-09-09), pinned by list digest `sha256:819ec9c063412e5730d1b0e82046ba540d1bf991f3c4f661a849aae8a0c52374`.** The first build whose GB10 GDN prefill runs FlashInfer's SM120 kernel (#55715, tested on a DGX Spark for correctness/perf only) and which contains the MRV2 workspace fix (#55341) and the b12x backends. It moves `chunk_gated_delta_rule` off Triton — the kernel the #37431 py-spy captures sat in — but leaves `causal_conv1d`, `fused_post_conv_prep` and the packed decode kernel on Triton, so it addresses one hypothesis, not the class. Env for both ranks: `VLLM_ALLREDUCE_USE_FLASHINFER=0` (avoid the new mnnvl init on a RoCE pair); run MRV2 as shipped first (it is what upstream now tests and what the 2026-09-07 two-Spark datapoint ran), fall back to `VLLM_USE_V2_MODEL_RUNNER=0` only if start-up or correctness fails. Expect the long-prefill regression (#53787) — measure the 262K/500K prefill and treat a > 1.3× slowdown as a fail for our 1M-window use. If Track A passed, keep A's flag on B only after B passes without it (one variable at a time). Newer nightlies (`2a02f6ef…` 09-10, `e7edf17c…` 09-11) are acceptable substitutes only if 385dce36 fails to start for a reason fixed later; do not chase `nightly`.
- **Track C (mitigation, last resort) — `--enforce-eager` on the pinned build.** The only arm that survived #54331's dissection (sm_120) and the config Xatter "can call stable" on GB10 for Qwen3.6-27B-FP8 (0.22.1), but on sm_121 for *this* model sequarell's graphs-off arm still faulted (compile on) and vlnofka saw hangs at pure-decode batches; cost measured elsewhere 8–34 % decode — on this pair, where FULL decode graphs were worth +45 %, expect worse. Test only if A and B both fail and the incident rate is unacceptable under the controller.

### 6.2 Rollback

`vllm/vllm-openai@sha256:24f2f8975d011ea7f7066a547886a08a1fd3c4bf0880463487fae4f01ce723c6` (image ID `60d84700e24f…` cached on both nodes). Insurance before any pull, because the tag is already gone and untagged manifests can be garbage-collected: `docker save vllm/vllm-openai@sha256:24f2f8975… | zstd -T0 > <backup>/vllm-openai-24f2f897.tar.zst` on **both** nodes (~22 GB each) — SRE's call. Track A rollback = revert the manifest line, `./techsara up`.

### 6.3 Switch procedure (files are owned by the SRE / launcher workstreams — described, not edited)

1. Pin sites carrying the digest: `compose/compose.dgx-spark.yaml:34` (`vllm`), `:173` (`vllm-router`), `:299` (`vllm-ocr`); `compose/compose.ocr.yaml:37` (`OCR_IMAGE` default); `config/model-manifest.yaml:578` (`images.vllm-dgx`); `launcher/techsara_cli/hardware.py:505` (GPU smoke-image preference list — cosmetic). **Recommendation: during a Track B trial change only the `vllm` service; leave `vllm-router` and `vllm-ocr` on the old digest** so the CONTRACT's fallback engine (the router) stays on the proven build and the trial has one variable. (Launcher tests under `launcher/tests` pin image strings — the SRE workstream should expect to adjust them.)
2. `scripts/cluster-sync.sh --image-only` — pulls the same digest on the worker (~10 GB arm64), fails if the image IDs differ, writes `CLUSTER_VLLM_IMAGE` into `worker.env`.
3. Under the engine lock (`scripts/lib/engine-lock.sh` / `scripts/cluster-recover.sh` per the new runbook): `./techsara up` (an engine-arg or image change is applied only by `up`/`deploy.sh --full`; routine deploys leave the engine alone). Expect a cold `torch.compile` (cache key changes with the build): ~5–8 min to `Application startup complete`; the controller's `COLD_START_BUDGET_S=900` covers it.
4. `scripts/cluster-verify-engine.sh --probe`: expect every check PASS except that "0 Xid lines in the kernel log since boot" already fails on Spark 2's current boot (tonight's Xid 13/31) — record the Xid count before the trial and require it **not to increase**; the script also asserts no `--speculative-config`, `--no-enable-prefix-caching`, byte-identical args on both ranks, NCCL `Using network IB`, and both GPUs ≥ 30 % during one completion. For Track B additionally grep the head log for `Using FlashInfer GDN prefill kernel (requested=auto, head_k_dim=128)` and, for Track A, `Using 'FLASHINFER_B12X' NvFp4 MoE backend`; assert the resolved `cudagraph_mode` and model runner from the engine banner, not from the launch script (sequarell's process note).
5. `orchestrator/.venv/bin/python scripts/cluster-soak.py --minutes 120 --concurrency 10 --worker techsphere@10.100.184.2` (the reproducer that fired at 27 min on 2026-09-11); JSONL under `.runtime/logs/`.
6. `orchestrator/scripts/validate_long_context.py --base-url http://127.0.0.1:8000/v1 --sizes 8192,32768,131072,262144 --max-output 128` (3 needles each), then `scripts/cluster-bench.sh` at c=1 / c=4 / c=10 and the 32K prefill.
7. Functional smoke through the orchestrator: a thinking turn, a tool-call turn, a `json_completion`, a vision turn (the main model is a VL model), a Deep Research step.

### 6.4 Pass criteria

- Soak `VERDICT: PASS` (zero CUDA-fault lines in both engine logs, zero new Xid on both kernels, zero container restarts, no frozen `vllm:generation_tokens_total` with requests running, every request answered) for the full 120 min; controller shows no `worker_rank_dead` / `wedged_frozen_tokens` / `canary_timeout` incident and `techsara_vllm_last_failure_category{category="none"} == 1` throughout.
- Verify script: all PASS bar the pre-existing Xid count; args identical on both ranks.
- Needles 3/3 at every size; 262K latency ≤ 1.3 × 78.5 s (Track B's known regression is a fail above that); bench within −10 % of 2026-09-11 (c=1 100.7 tok/s, c=4 238.7, c=10 283.9, 32K TTFT 4.10 s) — Track A/B may be faster on TTFT.
- Track A only: greedy outputs on the 20-prompt set materially equivalent to Marlin (no `!!!`/`<|box_end|>` degeneration, tool-call JSON valid), head `MemAvailable` ≥ 15 GiB at idle after warm-up.
- A 120-min pass is **necessary, not sufficient**: upstream MTBFs are 9–72 h. Declare the class closed only after ≥ 7 days of production with zero controller incidents of category ≠ `none` and no restart from a CUDA fault (Prometheus `changes(process_start_time_seconds{job="vllm-main"}[7d]) == 0` outside planned restarts); until then the controller and fallback stay armed exactly as for the pinned build.
