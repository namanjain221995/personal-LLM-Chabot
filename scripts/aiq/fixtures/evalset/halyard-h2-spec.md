# Halyard H2 Environmental Sensor: Product Specification

Synthetic test document. Fernleaf Instruments and the Halyard H2 are invented
for an evaluation set; nothing here describes a real company or product.

Document version 1.4.

## Overview

The Halyard H2 measures air temperature, relative humidity and CO2
concentration in indoor spaces such as offices, classrooms and warehouses.

## Measurement

| Quantity | Range | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature | -10 to 50 °C | ±0.3 °C |
| Relative humidity | 0 to 100 % | ±2 % |
| CO2 | 400 to 5,000 ppm | ±(30 ppm + 3 %) |

## Power

- Two AA lithium cells.
- Battery life: 14 months at a 5-minute reporting interval, 26 months at a
  15-minute reporting interval.
- A low-battery warning is sent when the cells drop below 2.9 V.

## Connectivity

- Radio: LoRaWAN, EU868 and US915 bands.
- Typical range: 2 km in open terrain, 300 m inside dense buildings.

## Environment

- Operating temperature: -20 °C to 60 °C.
- Ingress protection: IP54.
- Size 86 x 86 x 32 mm; weight 140 g including the batteries.

## Warranty

Three years from the date of delivery.
