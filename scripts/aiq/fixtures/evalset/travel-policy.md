# Brightwater Analytics: Travel and Expense Policy

Synthetic test document. Brightwater Analytics is invented for an evaluation
set; this is not any real organisation's policy.

Version 3.

## 1. Approval

Trips longer than 3 days need written approval from your manager before
anything is booked.

## 2. Flights

Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for
flights of 6 hours or more.

## 3. Hotels

Nightly hotel caps, including taxes:

| City | Cap per night |
|---|---|
| Berlin | EUR 160 |
| Lisbon | EUR 130 |
| Madrid | EUR 140 |

For any other city, ask the travel desk before booking.

## 4. Meals

- Domestic trips: EUR 45 per day.
- International trips: EUR 70 per day.
- Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## 5. Claims

Submit your claim within 21 days of the last day of the trip. Attach a
receipt for every item above EUR 25.
